Air taxis and flying vehicles, beyond airliners

They will not replace scheduled flights yet. They may add short urban hops in a few approved cities.

Home · Guides

Can you book one like a normal flight?

Not yet. Most eVTOL and flying-car projects are still in testing and short demonstration routes.

Who is building them

Look first at XPeng AeroHT, EHang, Joby, Klein Vision and PAL-V. Huawei is an AI and smart-mobility supplier, not a flying-car ticket seller.

How this differs from an airliner

  • Vertical or very short takeoff
  • Short city hops
  • Few seats and tight baggage limits
  • Weather, noise and city rules matter more
