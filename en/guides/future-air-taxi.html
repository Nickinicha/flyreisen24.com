Air taxis and flying vehicles, beyond airliners

Home · Travel Alerts

Can you book one like a normal flight?

Not yet. Most projects are still in testing and short demonstration routes.

Who is building them

Look first at XPeng AeroHT, EHang, Joby, Klein Vision and PAL-V, plus companies building navigation and air-traffic tools around those vehicles.

How this differs from an airliner

  • Vertical or very short takeoff
  • Short city hops
  • Few seats and tight baggage limits
  • Weather, noise and city rules matter more
