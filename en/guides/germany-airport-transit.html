What actually changed in 2026

Germany did not newly close its skies or publish a brand-new 20-country list this month. The list is long-standing. The real update is India: since 3 June 2026 Indian passport holders no longer need a category A airport transit visa to wait airside.

Thai passports

Thai nationals are generally not on the ATV list. Bangkok-Frankfurt-New York can work without a German visa if you stay airside. Bangkok-Frankfurt-Paris is Schengen entry, not airside transit.

Who still needs category A

Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Cuba, DR Congo, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Ghana, Iran, Iraq, Jordan, Lebanon, Mali, Nigeria, Pakistan, Somalia, South Sudan, Sri Lanka, Sudan, Syria and Turkey. Limited exemptions exist for Jordan and Turkey, and for holders of certain EU, Schengen, US, Canadian or Japanese visas or residence permits.

Five checks before you buy

  1. Does this passport need category A?
  2. Is the next flight inside or outside Schengen?
  3. Does the airport have a transit zone open at that hour?
  4. Will you collect bags or cross border control?
  5. Do you already hold an exemption document?
