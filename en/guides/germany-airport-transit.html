Germany Airport Transit Visa

A layover is not the same as entering Germany. Check passport, airport and destination before you pay.

Home · Travel guides · Travel Alerts

Most travellers can wait airside without a visa if the next flight is outside Schengen and they stay in the international transit area.

Nationals of 20 countries need a category A visa even if they never leave the terminal.

Who needs category A

Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Ghana, Iran, Iraq, Jordan, Lebanon, Mali, Nigeria, Pakistan, Somalia, South Sudan, Sri Lanka, Sudan, Syria, Turkey.

India has not needed this visa since 3 June 2026. Thai passports are not on the core list.

5 checks before you buy

  1. Does this passport need a category A visa?
  2. Is the next destination inside or outside Schengen?
  3. Does the layover airport have a transit zone open at that hour?
  4. Will a terminal change count as entering Germany?
  5. Do you already hold an exempting visa or residence permit?

Transit zones: Frankfurt, Munich, Berlin, Hamburg, Dusseldorf.
